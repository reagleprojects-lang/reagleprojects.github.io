Bitcoin Puzzle

An experience for exploring publicly published Bitcoin Puzzle keyspaces through cryptographic engagement and research-inspired searching.

Coming soon to the App Store
Overview

Direct engagement with cryptographic keyspaces.

Bitcoin Puzzle lets you generate cryptographic keys, derive Bitcoin addresses, and engage with publicly published target data through multiple search modes and exploration strategies.

This is a research and learning-focused experience. It does not promise or guarantee discovery of Bitcoin, financial returns, or rewards.

Core capabilities

  • Bitcoin Puzzles 71–160 exploration
  • Cryptographic key generation
  • Bitcoin address derivation
  • Random, sequential, and advanced search modes
  • Progress, history, and statistics
  • Not a wallet or custody service
How it works

Multiple modes for exploration and discovery.

⌁

Key generation

Generate cryptographic keys and derive their associated Bitcoin addresses for engagement with public puzzle keyspaces.

⊞

Search modes

Access random, sequential, skip/step, and range-based search modes available across different access tiers.

▣

Progress & insights

Track your exploration through progress metrics, search history, checkpoints, and statistics across your activity.

The Bitcoin Puzzle keyspace

Bitcoin Puzzles 71–160 represent extremely large cryptographic search spaces. Exploring them teaches about keyspace scale, search difficulty, and cryptographic principles.

Searching these spaces does not guarantee discovery of a private key, wallet recovery, or Bitcoin receipt.

Research-focused

Learn cryptography through direct engagement.

Bitcoin Puzzle is built to help you understand how Bitcoin keys are generated, how addresses are derived, and why keyspace size and search strategy matter.

It is not a wallet, exchange, payment processor, custody provider, or financial or investment product.

Feature areas

Designed for serious cryptographic exploration.

Important clarity

Not a financial product or guaranteed path to Bitcoin.

Bitcoin Puzzle is a research and exploration experience focused on cryptography. It is not an investment product, financial service, wallet, custody solution, exchange, or guaranteed method for earning or recovering cryptocurrency.

The experience does not request, store, or take custody of your Bitcoin private keys, seed phrases, wallet credentials, or cryptocurrency funds.
